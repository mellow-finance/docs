Mellow USDe+ is a Core Vault for USD stablecoins. Its user-facing entry vault is on Ethereum. Mellow is appointed to provide curation services for the vault.

Depositors receive mUSDep, the vault's share token, which represents their portion of the vault. Depositors do not manage any positions themselves. The strategy's results are reflected in the mUSDep price.

The vault is also deployed on Base, Monad, Plasma, Avalanche, Arc, Robinhood Chain, Ink and X Layer. Those deployments have no deposit or redeem queues, so all deposits and withdrawals go through the Ethereum vault.

## Deposits and withdrawals

Deposits are made on Ethereum in USDe, USDC or USDT. Each asset has a [SyncDepositQueue](/core-vaults/architecture/queues/syncdepositqueue), so mUSDep is minted in the same transaction as the deposit, priced at the latest oracle report.

Withdrawals are paid in USDe and take two steps. The depositor requests a withdrawal, then claims USDe once the request is processed.

A live [bug bounty on Sherlock](https://audits.sherlock.xyz/bug-bounties/125) offers up to 100,000 USDC for valid findings.
