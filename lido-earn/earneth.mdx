EarnETH is the ETH-denominated Lido Earn vault. It allocates ETH-based assets across DeFi strategies such as lending, leverage looping, liquidity provision and structured positions, within defined asset selection criteria and risk controls. Depositors receive earnETH, the vault's share token.

## Underlying vaults

EarnETH allocates across two underlying vaults, stRATEGY and GGV. Mellow is appointed to provide curation services for both. The split between them is rebalanced as conditions change.

## Deposits

EarnETH accepts ETH, WETH, stETH and wstETH. Holders of earlier Lido Earn tokens, GG, strETH and DVstETH, can deposit those tokens directly.

Deposits are typically processed within 24 hours. A pending deposit can be cancelled in the Lido interface before it is processed.

Deposit at [stake.lido.fi/earn/eth](https://stake.lido.fi/earn/eth).

## Withdrawals

1. Request a withdrawal from earnETH to wstETH.
2. Claim wstETH once the request has been processed.

Withdrawals typically take about three days. Payouts are made in wstETH. Withdrawal requests cannot be cancelled. Progress is visible in the Lido interface.
