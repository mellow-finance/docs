<Info>
**Legacy architecture.** Mellow-LRT is an earlier generation of Mellow vault infrastructure, documented for existing deployments. [Core Vaults](/core-vaults/overview) are Mellow's current vault architecture.
</Info>

Mellow LRT was Mellow's liquid restaking primitive, allowing permissionless creation of modular LRTs. Our smart contracts are built to offer a series of vaults tailored to different risk profiles, managed by curators.&#x20;
