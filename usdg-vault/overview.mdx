The USDG Yield Vault is a Core Vault on Robinhood Chain. It accepts USDG, the stablecoin issued by Paxos, and runs a capped stablecoin carry strategy with Morpho markets underneath. Mellow is appointed to provide curation services for the vault.

Users deposit and withdraw through the Mellow interface at [app.mellow.finance/vaults/robinhood-usdg-yield-vault](https://app.mellow.finance/vaults/robinhood-usdg-yield-vault). Depositors receive rUSDG, the vault's share token, which represents their portion of the vault. Depositors do not manage any positions themselves. The strategy's results are reflected in the rUSDG price.

## Deposits and withdrawals

Deposits are made in USDG.

Withdrawals take two steps. The depositor requests a withdrawal, then claims USDG once the request is processed. Withdrawals typically take 2–3 days.

## Security

A live [bug bounty on Sherlock](https://audits.sherlock.xyz/bug-bounties/125) offers up to 100,000 USDC for valid findings.
